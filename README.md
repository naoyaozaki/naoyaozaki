## 👋 Hi there, I’m Naoya Ozaki

Associate Professor and Mission Designer at the Institute of Space and Astronautical Science, Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA).

My research focuses on mission design and astrodynamics for deep-space exploration, with particular interests in:

* 🚀 Low-thrust trajectory design and optimization — developing efficient numerical methods, including Differential Dynamic Programming (DDP), for complex interplanetary trajectories.
* 🪐 Deep-space mission design — designing trajectories and mission architectures for asteroid and planetary exploration, including JAXA’s DESTINY+ mission.
* 🎯 Robust and stochastic trajectory optimization — applying optimal and stochastic control theory to trajectory design in the presence of uncertainty and operational constraints.
* 🤖 Machine learning for astrodynamics — leveraging neural networks, generative models, and other data-driven approaches to accelerate trajectory optimization and explore large mission-design spaces.
* 🌌 Multi-body dynamics and gravity assists — exploiting planetary and lunar flybys and multi-body dynamics to enable efficient and innovative exploration missions.

My goal is to advance deep-space mission design by combining astrodynamics, optimal control, and machine learning, making future exploration missions more autonomous, robust, and accessible.
